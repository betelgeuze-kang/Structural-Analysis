# Internal portal span comparison (predeclared input)

This bounded development study keeps the original three-member, two-fixed-base RC portal and the explicit v3 displacement-control request. It changes only the horizontal coordinates of both right-hand nodes (N2 and N4), moving the right column and its support together. The baseline beam span is 4.0 m; the alternatives are 3.6 m and 4.4 m. The load and target histories, sections, materials, supports, element connectivity, integration order, and solver settings stay identical. These are different geometries, not independent project validation cases.

The primary execution is a standalone `price_order` staged layout strategy with one target in each candidate prefix and a full-analysis budget of three including the baseline. An independently fresh full-path verification is required for each completed row. After the staged run, a separate `full` price-order strategy can calculate every model to expose whether a cost-pruned candidate also passes the full reference. Neither result qualifies a design or establishes speedup. The limits (all 1 in their typed units) deliberately provide a broad development screen. The common KRW price entries in `layout-span-comparison.json` are synthetic arithmetic, not market quotations or actual savings.

The staged plan may skip a more expensive alternative after verifying a cheaper incumbent. An unevaluated model remains physically unknown in that plan; the separate full run is reported independently. Preserve original outputs from any raised attempt and source-bind every run to its exact Git revision.
