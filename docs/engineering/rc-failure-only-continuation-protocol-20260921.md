The experimental benchmark adds `continuation_on_failure=True` alongside `frozen_parent_continuation=True`. It first runs the original native step. A known nonconvergence with exact rollback may trigger the fixed sixteen-stage proposal; an exception or unknown original work does not. The failed original invocation remains in the denominator. Successful ordinary steps execute no internal trial stages. The recovered seed receives one native acceptance attempt; no second copy of the already failed ordinary solve is added.

Both the initial deferred decision and subsequent continuation outcome have separate immutable artifacts. An initial test caught an attempted rewrite of the exclusive original proposal file; the recovery now writes its own file. Additional trial work and proposal time remain separately counted inside whole-path cost. The original-artifact replayer recognizes the distinct failure-only identity and preserves the failed first attempt. Pre-invocation residual observation is rejected for this mode because its proposal occurs after failure.
