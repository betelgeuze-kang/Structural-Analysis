## Source observations and open correspondence

The drawing specifies a 2,400 mm long beam with a 150 × 400 mm section, 2,000 mm support span, central 100 mm load plate, two Φ8 upper and two Φ16 lower longitudinal bars. It labels Φ10 stirrups, but the elevation and plan show 250 mm and 200 mm spacing respectively; their correspondence needs clarification. DMS 1 is drawn on the upper bar near midspan and DMS 2 on the lower bar. W1/W2 sit by the left support, W3 near midspan, W4/W5 by the right support, and W6 at the right end in the longitudinal direction. These drawing labels do not by themselves prove how the pickle's `DMS_*` and `WA_*` channels map to gauges or sign/datum conventions. The mix image specifies a design recipe (including w/c 0.60, CEM I 32.5 R at 350 kg/m³, and water at 210 kg/m³), not measured concrete strength or constitutive behavior.

Before any model correspondence or data admission, the source still needs the actual force convention (total machine force versus force at the load plate), the meaning of `Weg` and each `WA_*` channel, gauge locations/directions and calibration, time alignment, loading protocol, support restraints, bar cover, measured concrete and steel properties, and the observed cracking/failure mechanism. Three-point bending alone does not establish that shear, bond, or support effects can be ignored. The record declares CC BY 4.0 at dataset level; this pass did not establish separate per-file notices or third-party rights for embedded documents. Any later reuse must preserve source attribution, license, and transformation provenance. No original data, measurement values, fitted parameters, structural solves, or validation comparisons were added to the repository.
