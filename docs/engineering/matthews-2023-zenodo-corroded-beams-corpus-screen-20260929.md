# Matthews 2023 corroded RC beam corpus: scalar-source screen

This source-only screen of [Matthews, Palermo and Scott's Zenodo version 1.0.0](https://zenodo.org/records/8062007), DOI `10.5281/zenodo.8062007`, was performed on 2026-09-29. Its purpose is to decide what the compilation can support in the RC learning and physical-validation roadmap. **Newly admitted training specimens: 0; locked physical-validation specimens: 0; solver runs: 0.** The original CSV, PDF and numerical rows are not committed here.

The versioned record declares CC BY 4.0 for this **compiled dataset** and describes 804 independent beam tests from 54 experimental programs, including 649 corroded beams and 155 uncorroded controls. A read of the original CSV found 804 uniquely numbered specimen rows, plus 102 empty/separator records. Its corrosion-method codes divide those 804 rows into `IC=608`, `EI=30`, `N=11`, and `C=155`. The CSV has 80 header positions, 60 named; this file layout is not a count of model features. The user manual's Table 1 explains that some populated input and response fields depend on assumptions or calculations. The compilation's license does not grant rights to underlying paper PDFs, figures or original laboratory files; each original program still needs its own rights and provenance check.

The 155 control rows are not a homogeneous solver benchmark. Their configuration strings are `SS_FPB_MONO=115`, `SS-FPB-MONO=8`, `SS_TPB_MONO=19`, `SS_TPB_MONO_SUST=11`, and `SS_CONT_TPB_MONO=2`. The last two explicitly include sustained loading or a continuous beam; the underscore/hyphen variation is preserved, rather than silently normalized. Their recorded failure labels are `Flexural-bending (ductile)=127`, `Flexural-bending=3`, `Flexural-bending (brittle)=1`, `Flexure-shear=16`, `Flexural-shear=4`, `Bond slip=3`, and `Shear=1`. Thus 24 controls carry a shear/bond-related label despite the record's dataset-level description of flexure-dominated failures. Even the other 131 are **compilation labels**, not independently verified mechanisms or automatic admissions to the current Euler–Bernoulli fiber model.

## What the table can and cannot teach

The named response columns include `Py (kN)`, `Pmax (kN)`, `Mmax,exp (kNm)`, `Δy (mm)`, `Δult (mm)`, elastic stiffness and displacement ductility. These are scalar endpoints or derivatives. The released CSV and key contain **no ordered force–displacement path per specimen**. They therefore cannot supply full nonlinear-path validation, learned warm-start targets, iteration traces, reversal behavior or line-search outcomes. A later scalar-capacity study is conceivable, but only after choosing one endpoint with its original-source meaning and keeping whole experimental programs together across train/validation/test splits. The 804 tests are not 804 independent source programs.

The manual explicitly derives `MACI` from ACI 318-19 strain compatibility, elastic stiffness from `Py/Δy`, and ductility from `Δult/Δy`; it also documents assumed values for unreported material or corrosion descriptors. A populated `Mmax,exp` or material cell is therefore not, on its own, an authenticated measurement. For the 155 controls, a simple missing-value screen found one `Pmax` value absent/marked, 29 `Py`, 26 `Δy`, and 31 `Δult`. These counts are a file-level screen, not an endpoint-quality decision. Do not substitute a theoretical capacity, derivative or assumed material value for an experimental label.

The CSV's `Torres-Acosta et al. (2003), V01` and `V02` controls are the **same** numbered rows 161 and 162 already examined in the [original-paper crosswalk](torres-acosta-2003-zenodo-control-crosswalk-20260929.md). They must not re-enter as independent training or validation specimens through this corpus. That crosswalk found unresolved support span, steel provenance, response-event meaning and load-channel issues; its specimen-specific HOLD remains in force. Similar original-paper crosswalks would be needed for other candidate programs. The first physical screen should select ordinary, observed flexural controls that the present model can represent, then verify original geometry, support/load locations, material measurements, channel semantics and terminal mechanism before a separate admission decision.

**Decision: HOLD for model fitting and locked physical validation.** The compilation is useful as a source-discovery index and possible future scalar-endpoint cohort. It is not a full-path archive, a ready-made independent benchmark, or evidence that the solver or AI is accurate on 804 beams. Preserve the Zenodo version and file hashes, original program/specimen IDs, source-specific licenses, endpoint definitions, missingness and derivation flags in any future derived index. No numerical row is exported by this note.
