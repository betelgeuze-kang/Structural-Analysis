## Provenance and rights

- Muhammad and Tomlinson's [University of Alberta Biochar-Concrete Beam Test Results](https://doi.org/10.17632/znhx7sg9pk.1), Mendeley Data version 1, is the original data record. Its [public metadata](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/znhx7sg9pk/1) describes raw 2025 four-point bending data acquired under displacement control and names the linked MSc thesis for the specimen and setup details. The data record states **CC BY 4.0**; downstream redistribution of data or derived values would require attribution, a license link, and notice of changes.
- The linked [University of Alberta thesis metadata](https://ualberta.scholaris.ca/server/api/core/items/eccb2570-5776-45c7-b133-06de18676bd2) identifies Muhammad's *Experimental Evaluation of Biochar as Supplementary Cementitious Material in Reinforced Concrete*, DOI [10.7939/83345](https://doi.org/10.7939/83345). Its abstract describes nine 2000 x 300 x 200 mm four-point-tested beams: six detailed for flexure (three steel, three GFRP) and three steel beams without stirrups detailed for shear. These are program-level statements; the metadata does not map `SF-0B` to a beam or report its observed failure. The thesis rights field permits noncommercial access and restricts copying or reproduction without the owner's consent or an applicable legal exception. The thesis content was not accessed, downloaded, or redistributed for this screen. Its rights must not be replaced with the data record's CC BY license.

## Admission boundary

**HOLD.** Neither the open record nor the XLS explains the negative force/displacement signs, nonzero initial force, the `K` offset, whether either load channel is total actuator force or force at each nose, or whether the middle displacement is support referenced. No sign flip, zero shift, factor-of-two adjustment, sorting, or curve extraction is authorized by this note. The open metadata also does not give `SF-0B`'s verified specimen role, support span, load-point locations, bar arrangement and cover, measured concrete and steel properties, or observed terminal mechanism. The thesis abstract's program dimensions cannot fill those specimen-level fields.
