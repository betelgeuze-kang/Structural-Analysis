## Visual drawing/material review

The one-page PDF was rendered and visually inspected with the original mix PNG.
Text extraction alone captures almost none of the drawing annotations and would
have missed the geometry. Observed dimensions: total length 2,400 mm, support
spacing 2,000 mm, rectangular section 150 by 400 mm, central loading plate width
100 mm. The drawing labels two 8 mm top bars and two 16 mm bottom bars. Stirrups
are 10 mm, but elevation/section and plan show different 250/200 mm spacing
labels; that discrepancy is unresolved. Cover and exact bar-centroid offsets
are not established by this inspection. Support symbols do not alone establish
all restraint/friction conditions for a computational model.

The drawing places DMS 1 on the upper reinforcement near midspan and DMS 2 on
the lower reinforcement there. W1/W2 and W4/W5 are near supports, W3 near
midspan, W6 longitudinal at the right end. Associating pickle WA names with
these W labels still requires explicit channel interpretation; actuator `Weg`
must not automatically become specimen deflection. With span/depth 5, the
importance of shear must be assessed rather than assumed negligible from the
three-point-loading label.

The image is a mixture design, including w/c 0.60, cement 350 kg/m3 and water
210 kg/m3. It lists test specimens but no measured concrete stress-strain curve,
strength or modulus. Cement designation 32.5 R is not a measured concrete
compressive strength. Steel constitutive parameters also remain unknown.

The inspected rectangular section helper currently uses one `bar_area_m2` for
both top and bottom reinforcement; material quantities and learning features
also consume that common field. Exact two-D8/two-D16 reconstruction cannot be
claimed through that helper by selecting a single average bar area. A future
unequal-area extension would need consistent public input, section identity,
quantity/cost and learning contracts, not only a local fiber substitution.
No guessed material properties, cover or averaged reinforcement were admitted.
