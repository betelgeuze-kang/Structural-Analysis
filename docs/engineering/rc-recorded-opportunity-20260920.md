# Recorded opportunity in the existing seed proposals

The existing proposal family offers only a small arithmetic saving even with hindsight selection on the retained development labels. Improving the gate alone cannot create a larger saving from these same recorded choices. This diagnostic does not bound other proposals, new geometries or an actually executed switched full path.

The audit authenticated all 1,980 comparison reports and the fixed-tree validation scores against their pinned inventories. It checked completed arms, original parent and producing-policy identities, response agreement, reported work and three-repeat timing data. The 660 overlapping decisions represent 165 unique source samples, not independent projects. There are 32 consistently beneficial decisions from 14 unique source samples.

All 32 positive decisions reduce Newton iterations in every repeat. Their distribution is B=11, D=15, E=6, A=C=0, across the existing geometry/history groups. These are post-hoc descriptions, not case-ID features or selection rules to deploy. No held-out reserved case was opened.

An even more optimistic, unavailable per-repeat hindsight rule that takes whichever recorded arm happened to run faster yields 0.931618%, 1.274953% and 1.125666%. This second calculation can capitalize on timing noise and inconsistent benefits; it is not the original three-repeat label criterion and is not used to train or promote a model.

Both calculations substitute saved step times under the original parent state. They exclude gate feature/decision overhead and training, and do not propagate switched states. They are not full-path speed measurements, confidence bounds or promises of deployment savings. A separate sum over the retained rows reproduced the reported integer timings and work differences without additional fitting or solving.

Development implication: preserve deterministic secant, and require a stronger repeatable candidate-initialization benefit before further expanding gate complexity. Any new proposal must be developed separately from untouched project-level confirmation, keep the reference solver authoritative, and include full costs. The existing fixed tree remains unpromoted; this analysis changes no thresholds or labels.
