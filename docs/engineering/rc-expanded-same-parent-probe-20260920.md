## Next learning boundary

There are now beneficial and harmful examples across three synthetic families,
which makes investigation of a cheap pre-solve switching rule more grounded.
Do not select by observed secant iterations, elapsed time or target identity at
inference, and do not sum the best retained-parent times into a hypothetical
complete path. A switch changes subsequent parents and needs its own full-path
cost evaluation. If a switch is assessed by holding out a group, policies used
to create its training labels must also exclude that outer evaluation group;
the present pairwise-excluded policies alone do not prove that nested separation.
The reserved cases remain untouched and no independent generalization is claimed.
