# LeoPARD cast-beam candidate: distinct dataset license

[Sawicki et al. (2026)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1617/s11527-026-02945-5)
reports conventional cast and shotcrete-printed RC beams under four-point bending
and three-point loading. Reference 29 directly identifies dataset
[10.24355/dbbs.084-202506031524-0](https://doi.org/10.24355/dbbs.084-202506031524-0).
The article declares CC BY 4.0. Its monotonic, displacement-controlled four-point
cast-beam experiment is a candidate for evaluating the current flexural engine;
this is an inference about suitability, not a verified reconstruction. Printed
bent-up reinforcement, bond and shear cases are not interchangeable controls.

The successfully retrieved [DataCite record](https://api.datacite.org/dois/10.24355/dbbs.084-202506031524-0)
links back to the exact article and identifies the dataset license as
**CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**, distinct from the article license. Therefore article openness
does not establish commercial training permission for the dataset. Product
training admission remains false; no raw dataset files were retrieved.
The DOI/landing-page web reader was unavailable; metadata access succeeded
through the ordinary public DataCite API. No restricted access was bypassed.
