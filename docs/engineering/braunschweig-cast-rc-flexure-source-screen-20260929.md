# Braunschweig cast RC beam: source-only flexure screen

**Admission on 2026-09-29: zero measured evaluation rows and zero training rows.** This note records a new cast-beam lead and its decision boundary. The existing source-only records for [Mathern–Yang (#489)](https://github.com/betelgeuze-kang/Structural-Analysis/pull/489), [Zenodo 18735817 (#482)](https://github.com/betelgeuze-kang/Structural-Analysis/pull/482), and [Alberta SF-0B (#439)](https://github.com/betelgeuze-kang/Structural-Analysis/pull/439) remain separate; their paper/plot metadata, titanium-grout history, and original XLS audit respectively are not admitted rows.

The [Sawicki et al. 2026 publisher article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1617/s11527-026-02945-5) (Sections 2.1, 3, 4.1–4.2; Figs. 2, 6, 8; Table 3) reports a conventionally cast steel-reinforced beam tested in symmetric four-point bending and points to an independent [LeoPARD dataset, DOI 10.24355/dbbs.084-202506031524-0](https://doi.org/10.24355/dbbs.084-202506031524-0). The article is CC BY 4.0; the **separate dataset** [DataCite DOI record](https://api.datacite.org/dois/10.24355/dbbs.084-202506031524-0) declares **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** and describes synchronized applied-load versus deflection data at 1/8 Hz. The article's license cannot be substituted for the dataset's terms. Whether the contemplated downstream reuse satisfies the dataset's noncommercial and share-alike conditions remains unresolved.

| Admission field | Primary-source observation | Remaining gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Specimen and loading | The article's conventional cast beam is 3.4 m overall, 3.0 m support span, 160 × 300 mm section; four bottom 10 mm longitudinal bars, two top bars, and closed 6 mm stirrups at 250 mm. Its four-point force noses are 0.5 m apart. The paper treats that test as flexure-oriented; the distinct asymmetric three-point test probes shear. | Select only the cast four-point trace; verify as-built bar coordinates, cover, stirrup placement, concrete and bar properties for that tested beam. Paper design dimensions are not a specimen-specific survey. |
| Supports and history | The article describes support plates on rollers with mortar bedding and monotonic displacement control, 0.5 mm/min until steel yield then 1.5 mm/min. | Establish actual restraint freedoms, seating/bearing motion, load-nose reactions and full ordered load history before an exact solver setup. Two roller bearings in the paper are not automatically an ideal pin–roller pair. |
| Response endpoint | Section 3 says midspan deflection is the average of two beam faces, corrected with measurements over supports; Fig. 8 plots bending moment versus that deflection. The cast beam reached 47.9 kNm maximum moment, with compression damage near 55 mm deflection and later top-bar buckling near 143 mm. Dataset metadata promises synchronized applied-load/deflection samples. | Retrieve original publisher files and record filenames, hashes, column names, units, sign/zero conventions, support-correction formula, sampling/order, moment-to-total-load relation and any pauses or excluded intervals. Figure points and the metadata description do not supply an admissible numeric history. |
| Rights and access | The dataset's DataCite rights field is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. A direct request to the DOI-resolved [LeoPARD landing page](https://leopard.tu-braunschweig.de/receive/dbbs_mods_00078770) returned a `Security Check` HTML page on 2026-09-29, not data files. | Obtain ordinary authorized file access and clarify rights for intended reuse; do not work around the challenge or ingest a plot extraction. |

The [current public compiler](../public-rc-fiber-frame.md) admits a serial chain with one fully fixed endpoint; each section has one steel-material reference, one bar area and lumped top/bottom bar layers. It does not model shear deformation, bond slip or arbitrary support layouts. [Draft PR #439](https://github.com/betelgeuze-kang/Structural-Analysis/pull/439) includes an **experimental** pin–roller input path, which is unmerged and does not establish the cast beam's roller-bearing freedoms or physical agreement. Stirrups and bearing response still need a scoped modeling and validation decision. No solver run, fit, curve digitization, or measured-row admission follows from this screen.

The closest already byte-audited conventional flexure lead remains Alberta **SF-0B**, documented in [draft #439's source intake](https://github.com/betelgeuze-kang/Structural-Analysis/blob/fb41d86b592f2a874acedc4ae947f2a16b59fa6c/docs/engineering/alberta-biochar-rc-flexure-source-intake-20260928.md): the [Mendeley V1 dataset, DOI 10.17632/znhx7sg9pk.1](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/znhx7sg9pk/1) is CC BY 4.0, and the [Alberta thesis, Fig. 3.2, Tables 3.2–3.4 (PDF pp. 45–50)](https://ualberta.scholaris.ca/server/api/core/bitstreams/94c837c1-45dd-4174-86c2-f6094d34e21b/content#page=48) identifies a 2.0 m, 200 × 300 mm simply supported four-point beam with five bottom 15M bars in two rows (3+2), two top 10M bars, 10M stirrups at 75 mm through the shear spans, and tension-flexure failure. Its original XLS audit found a derived `Load Total` channel, separate machine and midspan displacement channels, and a failed strain channel. The dataset bytes and specimen sketch do **not** resolve force-correction meaning, each nose's force, displacement datum/support correction, specimen-specific bar locations, specimen-specific full 10M/15M constitutive histories, or the shear/bond contribution. The public section's one steel reference and lumped bottom layer cannot exactly represent the two steel-bar types and 3+2 layout. The draft pin–roller path may fit its nominal support topology, but these gates keep **all SF-0B rows out** of training and held-out physical evaluation.
