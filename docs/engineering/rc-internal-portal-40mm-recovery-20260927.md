# Plan-derived 40 mm portal: one fixed recovery path

The [portal study plan](rc-portal-recovery-admission-20260921.md) proposes amplitudes of 20, 40, and 80 mm with targets `(-A/2, -A, +A/2)`. Original request bytes are pinned only for 20 mm. Both observations below derive `(-20, -40, +20) mm` from that plan and use the internal corotational runner, its transformed three-member model, the original 20 mm solver settings, two fixed bases, -25 kN constant load at each roof node, and the +150 kN N4 horizontal reference load. They are **not** runs of an original 40 mm public request, a four-arm comparison, or independent physical validation.

Each observation started from fresh genesis, solved the same one-step `λ=0` preload, and allowed at most 16 fixed coordinate-continuation stages after a direct target failure. Every stage used the unchanged material parent; an intermediate stage's checkpoint was never adopted. Both plans declared a 90 s soft solver deadline, a 96 s worker timeout and a 100 s external cap. The old and new packets have different exact runner/driver sources and must not be combined into one timing result.
