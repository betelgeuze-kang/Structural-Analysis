# RC experimental physical-validation gate

**Status on 2026-09-29: HOLD.** This is a plan for comparing the RC flexure element and solver with real specimens, not a physical-validation result or training permission. The two pinned original workbooks contain **two specimens and 16,112 ordered measurement pairs**. They contain **zero admitted training rows, zero independent locked-evaluation specimens, zero blind predictions, and zero fixed-parameter solver comparisons**. Adjacent readings from one specimen are not independent examples.

This gate narrows [the dataset split policy](../validation-dataset-split-policy.md) and [the independent validation queue](https://github.com/betelgeuze-kang/Structural-Analysis/issues/297) to conventional flexural RC beams. A valid split manifest alone cannot establish that a load cell, LVDT, drawing, material test and solver output describe the same physical quantity. The source files remain outside Git; links and hashes identify them without copying experimental rows into this document.

## Comparison eligibility, in order

1. **Fix identity and rights.** Record versioned DOI/URL, publisher and author, original byte hash, specimen ID, laboratory and study ancestry, dataset-specific reuse terms and intended training/validation rights. Preserve the original row order and link every derived value to an original cell or instrument channel. Article reuse terms do not automatically govern a separately licensed dataset.
2. **Resolve physical channels.** Establish whether recorded force is the actuator total or each loading-nose force; name the LVDT and its support-reference correction; record units, signs, zero/preload convention, calibration uncertainty and any time or sequence meaning. Keep an unresolved conversion as `unknown`, never silently multiply force by two, shift an axis or remove reversals.
3. **Map the actual specimen.** Bind dimensioned support and load stations, restraint and bearing details, section and reinforcement coordinates, concrete batch/coupon results, steel coupon results and their uncertainty to this specimen. Label nominal dimensions, program averages and simulation assumptions separately. A parameter inferred from the measured response is calibration, not an independent measured input.
4. **Screen model applicability.** Declare the intended flexure-dominant range and check whether shear deformation/failure, bond slip, local bearing or support motion could materially govern it. The public axial-curvature fiber beam element does not establish those mechanisms. If they dominate the requested range, retain the specimen as out of scope until physics and its validation are added.
5. **Freeze a comparison packet before the first solve.** Pin source commit and executable hashes, all model inputs and parameter hashes, original/derived data hashes, selected specimen rows and their transformation algorithm, target solver path, units/sign mapping, uncertainty, failure treatment and numerical metrics with acceptance thresholds. Include the source-backed observable equations mapping solver nodal motion and reactions or load factors to the support-referenced LVDT and actuator-force channels, and hash that mapping. Include every attempted case in the denominator. The separate **internal research** direct-control path currently accepts at most 255 strictly monotone targets from its initial state; the He deflection has 74 adjacent decreases, while Chen's not-yet-identified `plot-x` channel has 5,610. Their full raw rows cannot be passed as a single direct-control target list. Any window, reduction, interpolation or alternate control choice must be declared from source/physics reasoning before seeing a solver score.
6. **Execute and report without promotion by omission.** Retain complete input/solver/output identities, rejected or incomplete paths, residual/iteration work, elapsed total cost and all specified response scores. Compare measured force–deflection quantities only after the channel crosswalk is resolved. Report uncertainty, discrepancy and unsupported intervals; a parser pass, synthetic fixture or visually similar plot is not a physical accuracy result.

He and Chen peak values and entire response histories have already been inspected during source intake. They therefore cannot be relabelled as undisclosed blind results. A later comparison with frozen inputs can provide a clearly named **retrospective development comparison** after the gates above, but does not by itself satisfy independent locked or blind service evidence. Do not fit on one specimen and count its load steps or another processing of that same specimen as a holdout.

## Independent evidence needed for the service objective

Before a new specimen's response is disclosed, a separate custodian or genuinely unrevealed source must register its study/specimen ancestry, rights, intended-use range and physical inputs; the solver team must freeze parameters, eligibility, metrics, thresholds and signed predictions. Predictions, including failures and out-of-range decisions, must be retained before opening the measured response. A separate study or institution boundary is preferable for generalization claims; same-beam repeats or row splits are not independent. The number and spread of held-out specimens must be justified against the intended service domain, including geometry, reinforcement, concrete/steel ranges, load history and dominant failure mode. No fixed specimen count alone grants service readiness.

The resulting evidence must show, separately, physical error and uncertainty across the declared range, the fraction of unsupported/failed cases, exact solver and AI-assisted total runtime versus the deterministic baseline, and an independent operator or lab review of the source crosswalk. Acceptance thresholds must be specified for the intended use **before** response disclosure and scored on every eligible case. Numerical validation does not replace design-code approval, licensing, support commitments or release decisions.
