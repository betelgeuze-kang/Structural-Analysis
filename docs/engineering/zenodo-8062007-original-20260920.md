# Original public flexural-beam table: provenance gaps narrowed

The official [Zenodo record 8062007](https://zenodo.org/records/8062007) and its
[API metadata](https://zenodo.org/api/records/8062007) were accessible on
2026-09-20. Metadata identifies **CC BY 4.0** and two files, whose advertised MD5
checksums and lengths both matched the downloads. This supersedes the earlier
landing-page access gap for this record. It does not establish a license for the
separate GitHub 725-row derivative inspected in the
[prior source note](public-flexural-beam-source-20260914.md).

## Direct original-file inspection

The pinned CSV contains 804 sequentially numbered specimen rows, 102 empty rows,
60 named columns and 20 entirely empty unnamed columns. No duplicate combined
author/specimen labels were found. Author-reference prefixes count to 54; they
provide leads for source grouping, not authenticated independent-project labels.

The explicit corrosion-method code `C`, defined as an uncorroded control in the
manual, identifies **155 controls across 51 reference prefixes**. A comment-text
search or zero mass-loss filter is not used to establish this count. Raw control
configuration strings are preserved: 115 `SS_FPB_MONO`, 19 `SS_TPB_MONO`, eight
`SS-FPB-MONO`, two `SS_CONT_TPB_MONO`, and eleven `SS_TPB_MONO_SUST`. Do not silently
collapse continuous or sustained-loading cases into ordinary monotonic controls.

All 155 controls have a nonblank experimental maximum-moment field, explicitly
headed in kNm. Maximum load is nonblank in 154, yield load in 126, yield
displacement in 129 and ultimate displacement in 124. These are scalar endpoints,
not ordered response histories. Availability is not verification of the value,
source extraction or experimental uncertainty.

The table now provides source/specimen names, dimensions, bar counts/diameters,
center-to-bar cover descriptors, shear span, material-strength fields and response
units absent from the earlier reduced table. No row correspondence between the
804-row original and 725-row derivative was assumed or fitted.

## Manual review and admission boundary

Text extraction plus visual inspection of manual pages 4 and 6 confirms several
imputed/derived fields. Missing water/cement ratios can be estimated, some material
categories assumed, and compressive test methods defaulted. Cube-to-cylinder
strength conversion is not applied in this table. Among controls, raw method
labels are 57 `Cube`, two `Cubic` and 96 `Cylinder`; do not treat all strengths as
one measured constitutive parameter.

`MACI` is theoretical capacity. Stiffness and ductility are calculated from
endpoint ratios. Keep these separate from measured labels and prevent response
ratios from leaking targets into input features. The landing description mentions
highlighted nonreported variables, but CSV cannot retain spreadsheet cell color;
per-cell measured/imputed status therefore requires original-source review rather
than inference from a populated cell.

This narrows access, record-level licensing, identifier and unit gaps. It does not
supply full load histories or establish material-law, bond, shear, support or
boundary-condition suitability. Controls remain discovery candidates. Corroded
rows do not enter the current uncorroded solver scope. No split assignments,
training rows, fits, structural solves or independent physical validations are
created. Future extraction must retain whole experiment/source families together
and trace selected controls back to their publications and specimen definitions.

## Reproducible inspection and retained bytes

`scripts/audit_public_flexural_beam_csv.py` requires the original CSV SHA-256,
rejects ambiguous named columns, nonempty unlabeled cells, changed row widths and
changed specimen numbering, and reports source/control counts without model
admission. It was executed against the original 804-row file; Ruff and diff
checks pass. No downloaded code, notebook or serialized object was executed.
